| 1 | title | A bet builder is one bet on several things happening in the same match. Every one has to win, and it's priced differently from an accumulator. |
| 2 | lines | Pick a match. Add selections from different markets: the result, goals, corners, cards, or a player to score. The site shows one price for the whole bet, and you place one stake. If any selection loses, the whole bet loses. |
| 3 | slips | In an accumulator, the selections come from different events, so the odds simply multiply. In a bet builder, they affect each other. If a team's striker scores, that team is more likely to win. |
| 4 | table | Say the team is two to win, and its striker is two and a half to score. Multiplied, that's five. But because the two tend to happen together, the bookmaker prices the pair lower, say at three seventy-five. On ten pounds, that's thirty-seven pounds fifty back if both win, not fifty. These prices are illustrative. |
| 5 | lines | Each extra selection lengthens the price, but it also cuts your chance of winning. A long bet builder pays well because all of its selections rarely win together. |
