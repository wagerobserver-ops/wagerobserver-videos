## Figures checked against the page

£5 at 10/1 returns £55; the place part at 2/1 returns £15; £70 back if it wins, £15 if it only places. At 3/1 with 1/5 terms the place part pays 3/5: £5 × 1.6 = £8. A place makes a profit only above 5/1 (1/5 terms) or 4/1 (1/4 terms), because 1 + odds/5 > 2 needs odds > 5.
