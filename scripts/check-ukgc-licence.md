About 85 to 95 seconds (about 220 words at 150 words a minute).

Four steps, not three as in the first plan: the page has four, and the video says the same. The page's worked example names real operators; the video uses an invented company instead (no operator names in videos), labelled «Example» on screen.

## Description

> How to check that a betting or casino site is licensed in Great Britain, in four steps: find the account number, search the Gambling Commission's public register, check the licence is active for the right kind of gambling, and check the site's address is on it.
