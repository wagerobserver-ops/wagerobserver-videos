## Figures checked against the page (23 Nov 2026)

All from /uk/guides/fractional-odds/ (same sums as `src/lib/odds.ts`):

- 5/1 on £10: profit £50, return £60; decimal 6.00 (5 ÷ 1 + 1).
- 1/2 on £10 returns £15; 4/6 returns £16.67 (10 × 1.6667); evens (1/1) returns £20.
- 6/4 is 2.50 in decimal.
- Implied chance: 3/1 → 1 ÷ 4 = 25%; 4/6 → 6 ÷ 10 = 60%; a market's chances add up to more than 100%, the extra is the margin.
- A £10 free bet winning at 2/1 pays £20, not £30 (stake not returned).
