## Figures checked against the page (23 Nov 2026)

From /uk/rules/wagering-cap-10x/ (and the calculator in `src/components/Calculator.astro`: total = bonus × multiple ÷ contribution; cost = total × (1 − RTP)):

- Cap in force since 19 January 2026, measured against the bonus: £20 × 10 = £200.
- At 96% RTP, £100 of bets costs £4 on average; £200 → £8; bonus worth £20 − £8 = £12.
- 35x: £700 of bets, £28 average cost, worth −£8.
- Roulette weighted at 30% (the Gambling Commission's own example): £200 ÷ 0.30 = £666.67, "about £667".
- Not changed by the cap: time limits, maximum bet rules, winnings caps (the page also lists minimum odds and free bets; left out for length).
